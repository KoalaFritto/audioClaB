# MAYDAY – Series bible (English · survival adventure + horror mystery)

## Creative direction
- **Experience:** open-sea survival with a real resource (water) that turns every choice into a gamble. The supernatural creeps in slowly and is never explained.
- **Different from ROOM 9:** an open space instead of a closed room, adventure instead of rules, and a **resource HUD** (water in litres, days to land, phone battery) instead of a clock.
- **Reward channels:** *curiosity* (a ship that sank in 1962 is calling for help), *prediction* (the water counter makes the viewer do the maths), *dilemma* (the bait, fresh water, is exactly what she needs).
- **Horror craft:** the radar is empty but the ship is there, and the ship stands still in the storm. That is concrete wrongness, never explained.

## Protagonist
- **Mara**, a solo sailor crossing the Atlantic on the *Petrel* (a 9 m sailboat). Faceless in the visuals, wearing a yellow foul-weather jacket. Her brother **Theo** follows her by satellite messenger.

## Resources (HUD)
- WATER: 40 L → 9 L after the tank cracks · LAND IN 21 DAYS · SAT BATTERY 63%.
- Survival rule of thumb: about 0.5 L/day at the bare minimum. With 9 L she makes it, barely, if nothing else goes wrong.

## Secrets (not revealed in Ep. 1)
- The *Elsa Marie* shows up every time someone is about to die of thirst at that spot. The water in the crates is real, but the ship "asks" for something in exchange.
- Theo's message arrives in time, but the signal is too weak to know whether the reply really comes from him (Ep. 3).

## Episode 1 beats
1. Day 3, the wave, the tank cracks: 40 L → 9 L (stakes)
2. The chart: 21 days to land (the maths of survival)
3. 2:47 AM, the radio wakes her: a mayday from the *Elsa Marie* (inciting event)
4. Radar: empty (wrongness)
5. Satellite messenger: "that ship sank in 1962. Don't answer it." (escalation)
6. Lights off the bow: the ship standing still in the storm (apparition)
7. Rope ladder + crates of FRESH WATER (the bait)
8. A: climb aboard / B: sail away with 9 L
