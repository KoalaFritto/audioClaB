# ROOM 9 – Series bible (English, interactive horror Reels)

## Creative direction (story-planning)
- **Experience for the viewer:** the cold, fun dread of "which one is real?", combined with the pleasure of a rule-based game where the viewer spots broken rules before the character does.
- **Format:** rule horror in the style of the popular Reels (night counter, phone UI, numbered rules, a binary choice voted in the comments). It's flat vector with faceless characters.
- **Why it works (reader reward channels):**
  - *Curiosity:* the gap "9 rooms on the sign, 8 doors" is never explained in episode 1.
  - *Prediction:* the rules are a contract with the viewer, who sees Nora break rule 3 without the narrator saying so ("trust the reader").
  - *Social simulation:* Nora is never described; she is understood through what she does (she lets the phone ring, locks the door, looks at the camera when she shouldn't).
- **Horror rules (craft/horror):** no explaining the threat, wrongness through small concrete details (the ninth ring, the name tag), no escalation on every beat. Silence comes before the voice at the door.

## Protagonist
- **Nora**, about 25, alone on the night shift at **The Pines Motel**, on a highway in the middle of nowhere. She took the job "because it paid double". Faceless in the visuals: dark ponytail, burgundy uniform, name tag reading "NORA".
- She is competent and cautious: she follows the rules, until the moment when following them means doubting herself.

## World rules (the manager's note)
1. Room nine doesn't exist. If it calls the desk, let it ring.
2. Lock the office at three. Don't open it for anyone.
3. If you see yourself on the cameras... look away.

## Planned truths (not revealed in Ep. 1)
- The "Nora" outside says she has been there "since midnight", which is exactly when the Nora inside arrived. Neither episode 1 nor the narrator says which one is real.
- Room 9 is not a room: it is whoever takes the night shift. (Season secret, not to be revealed before Ep. 4.)
- Night counter: NIGHT 1/5. Shift ends at 6:00 AM.
